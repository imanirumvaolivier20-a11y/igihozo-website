About us

A community initiative sharing handmade skills with children.

Why we exist

IGIHOZO Kids and Craft brings together crochet makers and other craft practitioners to share handmade skills with children. We offer constructive, enjoyable after-school activities where children explore creativity, practise patience and express themselves by making things by hand.

We also give local artisans a place to teach, strengthen their skills and work together.

Vision

A community where children discover their creativity, develop practical skills and grow in confidence through accessible handmade arts and learning.

Mission

To bring children, crochet makers and other craft practitioners together through safe, inclusive, hands-on learning that builds creativity and practical skills and encourages knowledge-sharing and community collaboration.

Our values

Creativity

Children and artisans explore ideas and make things their own way.

Inclusion

Welcoming and accessible to everyone, without discrimination.

Safety and care

Children’s wellbeing, dignity and protection come first.

Learning

We learn through practice, patience, feedback and sharing.

Teamwork

We work with families, schools, artisans and community partners.

Integrity

We use funds and materials responsibly and communicate honestly.

Sustainability

We look for practical ways to continue and to support instructors fairly.

What we do

Regular, supervised sessions for children and skill-sharing for makers.

Our activities

  • Crochet and craft workshops for children, after school and in holidays
  • Simple projects suited to each age and skill level
  • Demonstrations and exhibitions of finished work, with permission
  • Skill-sharing sessions for crochet makers and volunteer instructors
  • Work with schools, parents, community groups, suppliers and sponsors
  • A collection of handmade items that shows our skills and can help fund the work
